Connect your Arduino Uno + HC-SR04 and measure each item. The page calculates height and announces PASS or FAIL.

Use Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a computer. The website must be served over HTTPS (or localhost). Connect the Arduino by USB, then choose its COM port when prompted. Close Arduino Serial Monitor first.
